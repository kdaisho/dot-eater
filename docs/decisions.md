# Decisions

Settled decisions, grouped by topic. "Product decision" or "with the user" means the user decided: don't reopen it without asking. The handover (`docs/handover.md`) keeps only the decisions the next work touches.

## Gameplay and characters

- **Core** (product decisions): grid-based movement with smooth animation (not free movement); each character has a signature breath plus one stat; breaths use an energy meter refilled by eating dots; local 2-player has both co-op and versus; stages unlock in order.
- **Characters** (with the user): five animals in `roster.ts` (Dragon, Penguin, Owl, Skunk, Boar). The Dragon 🐉 is the default; the fly 🪰 is retired. The player sprite and the HUD lives are the playing character's emoji. Stars and par are the same for every character (revisit after playtesting).
- **Score and lives:** `DOT_POINTS` 10, `START_LIVES` 3. A collision with lives left costs one and respawns everyone at their spawns in the same tick, keeping dots and score. A collision on the last life is game over. Eating the last dot always wins, even if caught on that tick (product decision; in versus that catch still costs its dots).
- **Stars and bests:** 1 star for clearing, +1 for losing no lives, +1 for a clear time at or under the level's `parTime` (par rounded to whole ticks). Clear time is `GameState.ticks`, counted only by `step()`, so pauses and the ending outro don't count; shown as `m:ss.s`, rounded up. Bests are per stage in the v1 save as `stars` (max) and `bestTime` (min, ticks); merging with another tab keeps the better of each. Par times: 60, 70, 80, 75, 90 s (stage 5's to tune at preview).

## Two players (KDA-211)

- **State:** `GameState.players` holds 1 or 2 `Player`s (`character`, `breather`, `bufferedStep`, `score`, `eatenAt`, `rate`); `step(state, dt, moves, random)` takes one `MoveInput` per player (a single one is player 1's, the rest idle). Players move in order (player 1 eats a dot both reach on one tick), pass through each other and each breathe their own breath; breaths don't affect players. The game's score is `totalScore`; breath kills and the boss score for the player who breathed.
- **Targeting** (with the user): every enemy goes after the nearest player by maze distance from where it stands, over the cells as enemies see them, so a player a blocking tile cuts off counts as unreachable (`nearestTarget`; plain maze distance if neither can be reached; a phantom measures Manhattan, through walls); the boss the player it can get nearest, where its 2x2 footprint fits, then the one nearest its footprint by path now (`bossTarget`), so a player in a corridor it can't enter doesn't draw it from one it can reach. Ties go to player 1. With one player nothing changed.
- **Catches:** `GameState.mode` (from `createGameState(level, animals, mode)`, which `startLevel` passes) picks the rules, and `resolveCatch` in `game.ts` is the one place that applies them. Solo: a catch costs a life and respawns everyone; the last life ends the game. Eating the last dot wins whichever player eats it (on a boss level once the boss is defeated too), even with a player out.
- **Co-op catches** (KDA-221, with the user): the lives are one shared pool of `START_LIVES`. A caught player spends one and is taken out alone (`takeOut`): not drawn, no moving, breathing, eating or being caught, enemies and the boss ignore it, its input is ignored. After `PLAYER_RESPAWN_DELAY` (3 s, counted in ticks) it is back at its own spawn (`bringBack`), ready to move, facing right, nothing buffered, energy and score kept. Enemies, the boss, dots and the other player are not reset. Caught with the pool at 0, it is out for good (`respawnIn: null`). Two caught on one tick spend in player order. The game is lost once no player is in play or coming back (`outForGood` for all). While nobody is in play, every enemy and the boss head back to their own spawns (random enemies and patrollers keep their own ways), so none parks on a waiting player's spawn; once a player is back, they go after the players again. Fog lights stay where the players went out. In the HUD an out player's meter slot reads "back in 3/2/1", "back soon" while it waits for its spawn, or "OUT". Breaths never affect the partner (each breath tested in `game.coop.test.ts`). The 2-player pause screen lists both players' resume keys (1P P/Esc/Space, 2P Q/E).
- **Respawning onto an occupied spawn** (PM decision): a player that is due waits (at `respawnIn` 0) while any enemy in play or the boss is drawn within `RESPAWN_CLEAR_DISTANCE` (1.5 cells; the boss by its footprint's square) of its spawn, or is in a move that ends on the spawn or next to it (harmless enemies and a defeated boss don't count), and is back on the first tick it is clear. So nothing gliding in can catch it on the tick it is back or the next (checking only for a catch at once let an enemy 0.65 cells off catch it a tick later). No stage has an enemy or boss spawn within `RESPAWN_CLEAR_DISTANCE + 1` of a player spawn (tested), so enemies gone home never hold a spawn up.
- **Versus** (KDA-222, with the user): two players race for the dots on the same map; pure rules in `versus.ts`. Offered on the title as the third mode (`MODE_CHOICES`).
    - **Stuns:** a breath stuns the other player for `STUN_SECONDS` (2 s) instead of defeating it. One rule for every breath: the other player (in play) standing, by its cell, on any cell the breath strikes (the cells enemies would be hit on: the shape's cells plus the origin for a line or a cone) is stunned (`opponentsStruck`, called from `breathe`). So the Dragon stuns along its whole line even past an enemy it defeats, the Owl doesn't push the player, and the Boar's headbutt stuns a player in the cell ahead. A breath that stuns counts as fired even when its hook refused (the Boar with no wall to break), so it pays its normal cost and cooldown. Only the strike stuns: lasting tiles (fire, stink, ice) never do. A stunned player can't be stunned again until it is over (the timer doesn't reset), and once it wears off it is immune for `STUN_IMMUNITY_SECONDS` (1.5 s, with the user), so a breath fired whenever it is free can't keep it stunned; being taken out ends the immunity, as it ends a stun (our call). Stunned: its input is ignored, it finishes the glide it is in and, on ice, its slide (PM decision: only voluntary moves and breaths are blocked), and doesn't move on or breathe; it can still be caught, and going out ends the stun. Player 1's breath fires first, so on a tick both breathe at each other, player 1's stun stops player 2's breath. Never in co-op or solo.
    - **Catches:** no lives. A caught player loses `CATCH_DOTS` (5) dots, not below 0, keeps its score, and is out for `PLAYER_RESPAWN_DELAY` (3 s), then respawns as in co-op (`versusCatch`). A catch on the tick the last dot is eaten (or the boss defeated) still costs its dots and takes the player out before the round is scored (with the user; solo and co-op keep "the last dot always wins", free). A versus game is never lost.
    - **Dots and score:** `Player.dots` counts dots eaten, less catch penalties, and decides rounds; score still counts dots and breath kills (the boss's +500 too, which adds no dots).
    - **Match:** best of 3 on one stage. A round ends when the stage is cleared (all dots gone, and on a boss stage the boss defeated). Winner: most dots, then higher score; an exact tie is replayed and doesn't count (`roundWinner`). First to `ROUNDS_TO_WIN` (2) takes the match (`matchWinner`). Versus game screens carry `match` (the rounds so far); a round goes to `roundResult` (winner, dots and score per player, tally, and a Next round / Quit menu, `ROUND_CHOICES`: Next round plays on a fresh copy of the stage, same characters; Quit drops the match for stage select, still in versus, with the user) and the deciding round to `matchResult` (winner, a line per round, Rematch / Stage select). Both keep the `END_CONFIRM_DELAY` confirm lock (the cursor moves meanwhile) and follow player 1. Pause stays resume-only. Versus never reaches stage clear or game over and never touches saved progress or the last pick.
    - **HUD:** per player its dot count (`1P ●12`) in place of the score, and a stun countdown (`💫 2`) or respawn countdown in place of its meter; the round and tally (`Round 2 · 1–0`) in the middle instead of lives. A stunned player's sprite wears a 💫 badge, and an immune one a 🛡️ badge. The HUD under a result screen still shows the round as it was played.
- **Modes:** the title is the mode select; `Mode` is `"solo" | "coop" | "versus"` and every screen after the title carries it. `MODE_CHOICES` in `flow.ts` lists what the title offers, in order: `solo` ("1 player"), `coop` ("Co-op") and `versus` ("Versus"). Labels are `MODE_LABELS` in `main.ts`.
- **Character select (2 players):** each player moves its own cursor and confirms (`ready`); the game starts once both have. Both may pick the same character. Back from a confirmed player takes the confirmation back, otherwise it goes to stage select. Menus other than character select follow player 1; during a 2-player game either player pauses and resumes.
- **Gamepads** (with the user): in 1-player every pad drives player 1 (`"shared"`); in a 2-player mode pads take players in connection order, pad 1 player 1 and pad 2 player 2 (`"perPad"`). `padAssignment` in `main.ts` switches on each screen change: the title always shares, so any pad picks the mode; from stage select on the mode decides, so player 2's pad picks its character.
- **Progress** (with the user): 2-player games don't record clears, bests or stars (`recordsProgress`) or the last character pick, and can play any stage unlocked in 1-player.
- **Looks:** with two players each sprite gets a ring and its number in `PLAYER_COLORS` (blue, red); the HUD shows each player's score and meter in those colours with the shared lives (hearts) between; the end screens (stage clear and game over) show the combined score, then a line per player with its share and the dots it ate (`Player.dots`). Fog lights up round both players.

## Breaths

- **Energy** (KDA-208, with the user): the meter holds 100 (Skunk 150) and gains 5 per dot; it starts empty each stage and is kept when a life is lost. Costs: Dragon 50, Penguin 50, Owl 25, Skunk 50, Boar 75; no breath without enough energy. Cooldown 1 s after each breath (Owl 0.5 s). Breath kills score 50. A press that can't breathe is dropped, not buffered. A breath that refuses (`fire` returns `false`) spends nothing and starts no cooldown.
- **Defeated enemies** (with the user): respawn alone at their spawn after 5 s, blinking for the last 1 s; while gone they don't move, catch or show. When the player loses a life, every enemy goes back to its spawn in play with no statuses, a pending respawn included. Tile effects, energy and the cooldown carry over a lost life. An enemy coming back onto a player standing on its spawn can catch at once (the blink is the warning).
- **Facing** (with the user: starts right, breathing mid-slide allowed): explicit on the player (`Breather.facing`), the way it last moved, including slide steps and tunnels. At a move opportunity where every tried way is blocked it turns to the first one tried, so it can face a wall. It faces right again after a lost life. A mid-slide breath fires along the slide.
- **Statuses:** a held enemy stays where it is drawn (drawn without frame alpha), a harmless one doesn't catch, a solid one blocks the player like a wall and ends a slide next to it. Enemies still overlap each other.
- **Changing grids:** `GameState.ice` is the level's ice (`floorIce`) plus ice tile effects and is replaced, never mutated, when it changes, so `hasIce`'s per-grid cache holds. Opened walls change `cells` directly. Temporary ice never forms under a character.
- **The five breaths** (KDA-216–220; the numbers are tunable after playtesting):
    - 🐉 Dragon: line, range 5. Defeats the first enemy hit (an enemy on the player's own cell counts first) and stops there; the cells it passed burn 2 s (`dragon.burning`, blocks enemies, not the player). Refuses facing a wall with nothing to hit.
    - 🐧 Penguin: cone, range 3. Freezes enemies 3 s (holds, harmless, solid); covered floor becomes ice for 5 s. Refuses facing straight into a wall with no enemy on its cell. Stat (PM decision): "slides faster on ice", 1.5× speed on forced slide steps only (`BreathDef.slideSpeedScale`, applied through `GameState.playerRate`, set in `movePlayerTick`; the renderer glides the player at that rate). "Farther" was dropped: slides already run to normal floor or a wall.
    - 🦉 Owl: line, range 5. Pushes every enemy in it, one on the player's cell included, 3 cells along the facing; a 0.25 s look-only gust. Refuses when the line is empty and nobody is on the player's cell.
    - 🦨 Skunk: area, radius 1 (3×3 through open cells, the player's cell included), 5 s where breathed; blocks enemies. The 💨 centre is its own effect (`skunk.stink.centre`). An enemy already on the player's cell still catches.
    - 🐗 Boar: line, range 1. Opens the inner wall in front for 10 s (rubble look, no dot); refuses outer (border) walls and empty floor. Moves at 3.5 cells/s (`speedScale` 0.875).

## Breath contract

A breath file exports one `BreathDef` (types in `src/breath.ts`): `animal`, `shape` (`line`/`cone` with `range`, `area` with `radius`, or `self`), `cost`, `cooldown`, `duration` (informational), `maxEnergy`, `speedScale`, optional `slideSpeedScale`, optional `targetsWall(cells, wall)` (the breath acts on the wall its shape stopped at; the aim preview marks it), and `fire(hit)`. Return `false` from `fire`, before changing anything, to refuse.

`fire` gets a `BreathHit`: `origin` (the player's cell), `facing`, `cells` (the shape resolved against the maze, nearest first, never walls; walls stop it, tunnels wrap; never the origin), `wall` (the wall a line, or a cone's first cell, stopped at), `enemies` (enemies in play on `cells`, nearest first; a line or cone also lists enemies on `origin` first), `state` (read only), and the effects:

- `defeat(enemy)`: +50; the enemy waits at its spawn, out of play and not drawn, blinks for the last 1 s and is back after 5 s (a patroller restarts its route). A whole Splitter splits instead.
- `addStatus(enemy, status, seconds)`: `EnemyStatus` flags `holds`, `harmless`, `solid`, and a `look`. The same `name` again refreshes to the longer time.
- `addTile(pos, effect, seconds)`: `TileEffect` flags `blocksEnemies` (every AI, phantoms too, treats it as a wall; an enemy inside leaves by the shortest way; a player or patrol waypoint inside still draws enemies to the patch's nearest edge), `ice` (never under a character; the ice grid is rebuilt once after `fire`), `opensWall` (only on a wall; grows back when it ends, pushing anyone inside back where they came from, else to the nearest open cell, never under a boss in play), and a `look`. Tiles dedupe by `name` and position. A look-only tile is a pure visual.
- `push(enemy, step, distance)`: up to `distance` cells, through tunnels, stopping before walls (not at `blocksEnemies` cells); it glides in over the last cell, so it slides on if it lands on ice. Collisions that tick start from where it lands. If the glide-in would start where the player is drawn (its cell or `lastPos`), the enemy is drawn on its new cell at once and doesn't slide, so a push never catches on its own tick.

The boss (KDA-207) is outside the contract: it isn't in `enemies`, so `defeat`, `addStatus` and `push` never reach it. The engine (`breathe`) damages it by `cost / 25` once per breath when any struck cell is under its 2×2 footprint, and then the breath counts as fired even if `fire` refused. A line or cone ends at its body: its cells there are in `cells`, cells behind it aren't. For a cone, behind means further out in any lane (offset across the facing) that met the body; cells in the lanes beside it are still reached.

`Look` is `{ color?, emoji? }`: for a tile, a fill under any dot plus an emoji on top; for an enemy, a disc behind the sprite plus a small badge. Give each status and effect a unique `name` such as `"dragon.burning"`. Test with `makeState(..., { breath })` and `MoveInput.breath: true` (`src/game.breath.test.ts`, `testBreath` in `src/test/helpers.ts`, one `src/breaths/<animal>.test.ts` per breath).

Order inside `step`: breath timers count down (expiries, regrowth, respawns), the player moves (setting its facing), the breath fires from the player's new cell, enemies' tick starts are re-read, enemies move, then collisions.

## Enemies

- **Types:** up to 4 enemy slots (`ENEMY_IDS`). An unknown AI type throws. Sprites follow the AI type (`ENEMY_SPRITES`): random 👺, chase 👹, ambusher 🦊, patroller 🤖, phantom 👻, splitter 🦠.
- **Chase AI** scores each forward candidate by BFS distance to the player with the enemy's own cell blocked, so the no-reverse rule can't send it into a dead end. Ties go to direction order; an unreachable player falls back to the first candidate. On levels with ice the distance is slide-aware (`slideDistances` in `ice.ts`); without ice (`hasIce`) it uses plain `distanceMap`. Blocked tiles: enemies move on `blockedView` (blocked cells are walls) but measure distance on `goalView`, which reopens the blocked patch containing the goal, so they head for its nearest edge.
- **Ambusher** aims `AMBUSH_AHEAD` (4) cells ahead along the player's latest move (straight line, no wrap; walks back to the first open cell, else the player) and reuses chase scoring.
- **Patroller** follows a looped `route: [[x, y], ...]` by shortest path, may turn back, restarts the route on respawn; pathing is slide-aware (KDA-230).
- **Phantom** ignores walls, stays on the map, doesn't use tunnels, defaults to `PHANTOM_SPEED` (1.5), never slides.
- **Splitter** (KDA-229, with the user): `ai: "splitter"`, chases at the default enemy speed. Only `defeat` splits it (+50), through `defeatEnemy`, so every defeating breath gets it. Two halves on its cell chase at `SPLITTER_HALF_SPEED` (2.5), drawn at `HALF_SCALE` (0.65), never split again, +50 each, and don't respawn alone; when both are gone it is back whole at its spawn after 5 s with the blink. A lost life resets it to one whole Splitter. It owns two adjacent enemy slots (the spare is out of play with `respawnIn = GONE`, Infinity), so `parseLevel` counts it as 2 toward the cap. Spreading: halves go to the two open neighbours farthest from the player, never onto the player's cell or `lastPos`; in a dead end they share a cell; with no free neighbour they go to the nearest free cells further out; if the Splitter stood on either player cell they start at rest. Guarantee: a split never catches the player on the split tick or right after.

- **Boss** (KDA-207, with the user; `boss.ts`): one per level at most, `GameState.boss`, not an enemy slot. 2×2 (`pos` is the top-left cell), health 12, damage `cost / 25` per breath (Owl 1, Dragon/Penguin/Skunk 2, Boar 3, the Boar even with no wall in front), white flash 0.15 s per hit. Can't be frozen or pushed; `blocksEnemies` tiles block it (it leaves by the shortest way), and it treats Boar-opened walls as walls. Chases at 1.5 cells/s through space where its whole footprint fits: it heads for a position covering the player, else the one nearest the player by path (it waits at a corridor's mouth), staying put on ties. Catches on any touch of its drawn footprint (closest approach to the square of its four cell centres within `CATCH_DISTANCE`). A lost life sends it to its spawn, damage kept. At 0: gone for good, +500, one big confetti burst. With the dots gone and the boss alive, energy refills 10/s. A boss level is won when the dots are gone and the boss is defeated, whichever comes last, even if caught on that tick.

## Movement, input and collisions

- **Speeds:** player 4 cells/s, enemies 2 cells/s by default (applied only in `createCharacter`); levels may override `playerSpeed` and per-enemy `speed`.
- **Move progress** keeps the leftover past 1 when a character moves, so speeds that don't divide 60 Hz average out. A character that is ready but can't move waits at exactly 1. The player starts ready (`moveProgress` 1); enemies start at 0, a short grace period.
- **Input:** keys are matched by `KeyboardEvent.code`. P1: arrows, ShiftRight breath, P/Escape pause, Space confirm. P2 (KDA-211): WASD, ShiftLeft breath, Q pause, E confirm; both maps are always attached, and flow reads player 2 only in a 2-player mode. Auto-repeat never presses; a missed keyup or keydown recovers on the next keydown. A direction held when play starts from a menu (character select or Retry) is ignored until pressed again (`InputState.ignoreHeld`); resuming from pause isn't affected.
- **Remapping** (KDA-215, with the user): those maps are the defaults (`DEFAULT_BINDINGS` in `bindings.ts`). Per player, each of up, down, left, right, breath, pause and confirm has one key, and breath, confirm and pause one pad button; the pad's D-pad and stick stay fixed. Escape always pauses P1 and goes back, and can't be bound; nor can Ctrl, Alt, Meta, CapsLock, Fn, NumLock, ScrollLock or the IME keys (KanaMode, Lang1, Lang2, Convert, NonConvert). Binding a key in use swaps the two keys, across players too. Pad buttons swap within a player only (each player has their own pad). Breath and confirm may share a button (A by default); pause never shares, so moving onto pause's button, or pause onto theirs, swaps the two buttons whole (confirm onto Start with A shared: breath and confirm on Start, pause on A). Stored bindings fall back per entry (a key must look like a `KeyboardEvent.code`: `/^[A-Z][A-Za-z0-9]+$/`), then all keys reset if two actions share a key, and a player's pad resets if pause shares a button. Unknown fields in stored bindings are kept, like the settings' own. A key or button held when bindings change presses its new action only once pressed again. **Controls screen** (title path only: Settings → Controls): one player at a time, P1 first; up/down move, left/right switch between 1P and 2P. Rows: Up, Down, Left, Right, Breath, Pause, Confirm (keys), Pad breath, Pad confirm, Pad pause (buttons), Reset to defaults, Back. Confirm on a row prompts "Press a key/button for …"; a key row takes a key, and a pad button there cancels (so a pad-only player can get out), unless a key came on the same tick; a pad row takes a button (any pad, in the title's shared assignment), and a key there keeps it waiting. Main keeps the first key and the first button apart, so a stray press of one kind never hides the other. Escape cancels on either. While waiting, every action press is ignored, and the captured press never acts on the menu. Every change is saved (`settings.bindings`, optional, checked with `parseBindings`) and applied at once to the keyboard, the pads and the hints. Reset to defaults drops the bindings from the settings, so they follow the defaults from then on. Back or the pause action returns to Settings on Controls. The on-screen hints name P1's (and in 2-player hints P2's) bound keys; Escape is always "Esc".
- **Tap buffer:** the newest freshly pressed direction is kept in `GameState.bufferedStep` and tried first at the next move opportunity, then the held directions (`MoveInput.held`, newest first); cleared at that opportunity either way.
- **Collisions** (KDA-231, product decision) are on drawn positions (`drawnPos`: `lastPos` to `pos` by `moveProgress` capped at 1, no frame alpha). The player is caught when an enemy comes within `CATCH_DISTANCE` (0.6; sprites are `SPRITE_SCALE` 0.8 wide) at any moment of the tick, exact and wrap-aware (`wrapDelta`). Any touch is a catch, including from behind (product decision). Enemies aim at the player's start-of-tick cell; a chaser on the player's logical cell aims at its `lastPos`.
- **Ice:** a separate ice grid beside the cells. A character slides when it stands on ice, its latest move was one cell and the next cell that way isn't a wall (or a solid enemy, for the player); it moves that way on its move turns, ignoring input and AI, and stops on normal floor or against a wall. Slides go through tunnels and eat dots. A mid-slide tap stays buffered until the first free move.

## Levels, stages and screens

- **Levels:** typed `.ts` modules exporting JSON-serializable `LevelData`. Map characters: `#` wall, `.` dot, space empty, `~` ice, `:` ice with a dot, `P` player 1 spawn, `Q` player 2 spawn (optional, at most one; without it player 2 starts on `P`; every stage has one right of `P`; it holds a dot, which `createGameState` clears only when there is a player 2, so 1-player keeps every dot), `E` enemy spawn (starts on a dot), `B` boss spawn (top-left of its footprint, a dot; the other three cells `.` or space; optional `boss: { speed }`). Enemies pair with `E`s in reading order. A boss level has at most one `B`, no wrap tunnels and no ice. The map is the only source of dots. `parseLevel` rejects: no dots, unreachable dots, an open edge cell that wraps into a wall, wrong spawn counts, more enemy slots than 4 (a Splitter counts 2), bad patroller routes, speeds above `TICK_RATE`, a `parTime` that isn't positive whole tenths of a second, bad `rules` (unknown keys, fog radius below 1.5), and any reachable state from which sliding can strand the player (checked from every player spawn).
- **Stages:** stage 1 "Classic" replaced the old 9x9 level 1, kept as the `OLD_LEVEL` test fixture (map-dependent tests use it, not `levels[0]`). Stage select always shows `STAGE_COUNT` (5) slots: open, locked ("🔒 Name") or "Coming soon"; the cursor lands on locked slots but confirm does nothing there.
- **Progress:** `{ version: 1, stages: [{ cleared, ...extra }] }` under `dot-eater:progress:v1`; bad JSON, another version or a wrong shape resets to empty (no migrations). A win records the clear via `recordClear`, which merges what storage holds first; a loss saves nothing.
- **Screens** (product decision): title → stage select → character select → play. Back is the pause action: character select → stage select (same stage) → title. Character select shows one card per `ANIMAL_IDS` entry; ←/→ move, stopping at the ends; Space plays. The cursor starts on the last pick (plain id under `dot-eater:character:v1`; unknown → Dragon). Every game screen carries `animals` (one per player) and `mode`, so Retry keeps them and skips character select. Retry is pre-selected on both end screens. Only `playing` steps the game. Each screen change uses a whole tick and presses are cleared every tick, so menu presses never reach the game.
- **End of a game is seen, not jumped to:** the dot just eaten stays drawn (`GameState.eatenAt`) until the player's glide is halfway onto it, and a finished game goes through `ending` (only `settle()` runs) before the end screen and confetti.
- **Auto-pause:** during play, window `blur` or a hidden tab switches to paused.

## Rendering

- Characters glide from `lastPos` to `pos`, lagging at most one cell behind the logic; wrap tunnels slide (drawn on both edges, clipped). The HUD strip is above the maze: score left, lives right, energy meter in the middle (a mark per breath's cost, amber when ready). Breath looks come from breath data; `render.ts` has no per-breath code.
- **Fog:** `rules.fog.radius` (at least 1.5) is purely visual: things wholly outside the radius around the player's glide position aren't drawn, with a 1.5-cell soft edge; the HUD stays visible; wrap-aware with tunnels.

## Polish

Decided with the user on 2026-10-27 (KDA-212–215, KDA-246):

- **Sound** (KDA-212): all sound is synthesized with Web Audio in a chip style, with no asset files; each stage has its own generated music loop.
    - **Unlock:** audio starts on the first key press or tap (`audio.unlock()`, the browsers' autoplay rule). Effects played before that are dropped; the tune asked for starts then. A gamepad press doesn't count as a gesture in browsers, so a gamepad-only player hears nothing until a key or tap.
    - **Music:** the title tune on the title, the menus and settings opened from the title; the stage's tune (`stageTrack`) while playing. Pause (settings opened from pause included) freezes the tune, and resuming carries on from the same point. From the ending outro on there is no music, so the stage clear and game over jingles play alone, and the result screens are quiet.
    - **Effects:** one per game event (`soundForEvent` in `sound.ts`): a breath sound per animal, a dot blip, enemy defeated, boss hit and defeated, caught, stunned, stage clear (also a won versus round), game over. A respawn makes none, so a solo catch is one sound; each sound plays at most once per tick, and game over replaces the catch that lost the game. Menus blip on cursor moves, confirm, back, and pause/resume.
    - **Volumes:** Music, Effects and Mute from settings apply live, from the pause screen too. Gain is (volume / 10)²; music at full volume sits at half the effects' level.
- **Settings** (KDA-246): a Settings screen opens from the title menu (after the modes) and from the pause menu (Resume, Settings); Back returns where it came from, a paused game untouched. Remapping is on the title path only. Rows: Music volume and Effects volume (0–10 each), Mute (master), Screen shake; KDA-214 adds Show D-pad and KDA-215 Controls.
- **Reduced motion** (KDA-213): with reduced motion requested, there is no screen shake and particles are fewer and shorter. The Screen shake toggle defaults to off under reduced motion and on otherwise (unset, it follows the system).
- **Touch** (KDA-214): touch drives player 1 only, and every menu is tappable. Swipe is the default; an on-screen D-pad is off by default, with a Show D-pad toggle shown only on touch devices.
    - **Swipes stick** (PM call, to try on a phone): a swipe turns the player and stays held after the finger lifts, and the previous swipe stays held behind it as a fallback, so an early turn waits for its junction and the player follows corners; a swipe back the way it came drops the fallback. Re-swiping the held direction presses it again. One finger can keep steering without lifting. A direction pressed by a key, pad or the D-pad drops the swipe (`PlayerInput.latch`), so the newest input wins and letting go stops the player; a game starting or ending (not pause/resume) lets go of the swipe too.
    - **Thresholds** (`touch.ts`, `dpad.ts`): a swipe is 24 CSS px (`SWIPE_DISTANCE`); a tap moves at most 12 px (`TAP_SLOP`) and lasts at most 400 ms (`TAP_MAX_MS`) and breathes on lift; the D-pad's dead zone is 10 px from its centre. Mouse input on the board is ignored.
    - **⏸ button:** during play on touch devices (`isTouchDevice`: a coarse pointer, or touch points), top right, since a phone has no Esc.
    - **Menus:** one tap on an item or card puts the cursor there and confirms (`selectItem`, then a confirm press), so the end screens' confirm delay still applies; a slider's ◀ and ▶ step it; Back buttons on stage and character select; the pause screen is its Resume/Settings menu; on the controls screen a tap runs a row, and while it waits for a key or button any tap on the overlay cancels, like Escape (the prompt reads "Tap to cancel" on touch devices). Key-only hints, the controls screen's included, are hidden on phones; the capture prompt itself always shows (`(hover: none) and (pointer: coarse)`).
    - **D-pad:** DOM buttons (`dpad.ts`), not drawn on the canvas, so each control has its own pointer events and CSS places them. With it on, the maze leaves a 180 px band (`DPAD_SPACE`, `controlsSpace`): below it in portrait (D-pad left, breath right), on both sides in landscape. The D-pad holds a direction while touched and follows the finger round it; it shows only on touch devices with Show D-pad on, applied live.
- **Remapping** (KDA-215): per player, one key each for up, down, left, right, breath, pause and confirm, plus pad buttons for breath, confirm and pause; the pad's D-pad and stick are fixed. Escape always pauses and backs out and can't be rebound. Binding a key already in use swaps the two bindings, across P1 and P2 too. Reset to defaults restores them all. Details, including pad sharing and the controls screen, are in the Remapping bullet under "Movement, input and collisions".
- **Game events** (KDA-246): `step` reports what happened in each tick as `GameState.events` (`GameEvent` in `events.ts`: dots, breaths, defeats, boss hits, catches, respawns, stuns, win and loss), one event per occurrence. Main takes them after each tick (`takeEvents`) and hands them to `onGameEvents`, where sound and effects hook in.
- **Particles and shake** (KDA-213; looks and amounts are placeholders to tune at preview):
    - Visual only: `particles.ts` and `shake.ts` are pure, and nothing in the game reads them. `eventEffects.ts` maps each game event to effects. A dot eaten gives crumbs. A breath gives its own look (`BreathDef.particles`) on every cell its shape covers, flying along the facing. An enemy defeated gives a defeat burst and a hit shake. A boss hit or defeat gives a burst at the footprint's centre and a shake. A catch gives a red burst and the caught shake. A versus stun gives a small shake. Respawns, wins and losses get nothing.
    - The shake moves the maze inside its clip; the HUD and the fog's darkness stay still. The offset is in cells (up to `MAX_SHAKE` 0.25 cell), by trauma squared; trauma drains at 2.5 per second.
    - Effects move with the game's ticks, so they freeze while paused. A new game starts with none. Particles show during play, pause and the outro, and are hidden on end screens. They are hidden outside the fog's light.
    - Reduced motion: particles are 35% as many (at least 1), with half the life and half the speed. The shake is `shakeEnabled(settings, reduced motion)`, recomputed on load, on every settings change and when the system setting changes.
- **Aim preview** (KDA-247, with the user): while a player's breath is ready (the meter's amber rule, `canBreathe`), it is in play and not stunned, and only on the playing screen (not paused, ending or end screens), the cells its breath would hit get a faint tint in its `PLAYER_COLORS` colour (blue P1, red P2; P1 blue in solo too) with a slightly stronger edge round the shape, drawn under the dots and characters and hidden outside the fog's light. The cells come from `aimCells` in `breath.ts`, which `breathe` shares: the shape from the player's logical cell along its facing (right before it has moved), stopped by walls and the live boss's body, wrapping through tunnels; plus the wall the shape stopped at when the breath acts on it (`BreathDef.targetsWall`: the Boar's breakable wall). A breath that would reach nothing (a line facing a wall) shows nothing. Both players' previews show, overlapping. Alpha 0.18 fill, 0.5 edge, to tune at preview.
- **Settings storage** (KDA-246): `{ version: 1, musicVolume, effectsVolume, muted, screenShake, showDpad }` under `dot-eater:settings:v1`; defaults 7, 8, off, unset (`null`), off. Bad JSON, another version or a non-object gives the defaults (no migrations); an invalid field on its own falls back to its default, and unknown fields are kept. New settings (KDA-215's bindings) are added as optional fields without a version bump.
